Bernard was patiently waiting for me to read some pieces of **impenetrable prose** that he had **dug up**, **in answer to my question I had asked** him yesterday: what are my actual powers **in various far-flung parts** of the UK, such as Scotland and Northern Ireland.

> provision

**It is  proposed that** ,

> 1. 虚拟语气
> 2. 被动句式

**Insofar as** the **implementation** of the statutory provisions **is concerned**,

> Insofar as ... is concerned = as far as ... is concerned.

> statue 是雕像

> [[fix -ory -atory]]
> verb+ `-ory`
> explain -> explanatory

**"A catastrophe. A tragedy. A cataclysmic, apocalyptic, monumental calamity."**
> 押韵了
> **cataclysmic**：源自古希腊语“大洪水/地壳巨变”
> **apocalyptic**：源自《圣经·启示录》中的“世界末日”
> **calamity**：源自拉丁语，指历史性的浩劫。

You've seen this abominable speech?
> Abominable **极其恶劣的、可恶的、糟糕透顶的、令人憎恶的**。它用来修饰那些在道德上让人反感、在质量上极其恶劣，或者让人感到极度不适的事物。
> terrible（糟糕的）, detestable（可憎的）, atrocious（残暴的/极坏的）, loathsome（讨厌的）。
> detest 谴责，诅咒
> 1. 前缀 **de-**：意为 **"down"（向下、贬低、彻底）**。
> 2. 词根 **testari**：意为 **“作证、证实”**（源自 testis 证人）。
