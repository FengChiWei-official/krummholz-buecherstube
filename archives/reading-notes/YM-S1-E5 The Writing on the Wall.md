## Text

The Writing on the Wall
> The bible story: 
> 是危机的预兆的意思

Don't quibble, Bernard.
> 不要咬文嚼字

Stop wittering.
> 别唠叨了

a totally unintelligible report
> unintelligible 不知所云的， 指胡说八道/癫狂的文字
> impenertrable 深奥的，指有条理，太过于难以理解的文字

As long as you are not asking me to **resort** to **crude generalizations** or **vulgar over-simplifications**, such as a simple yes or no, I shall do my utmost to oblige.
> crude generalizations 粗俗的概括
> vulgar over-simplifications 的过度简化
> do my utmost to do, 相当于 do my best to
> oblige 
> 被迫
> I was obliged to do sth
> 帮助/效劳
> I'd love to oblige
> happy to oblige
> I would like to oblige you with sth
> 感谢
> be obliged to you for sth

Looking at it by and large
> 全局来看
> by and large
> by 指“迎风行驶”（close-hauled）；
> large 指“顺风行驶”（sailing with the wind off the quarter）；

**Not to put too fine a point on it**, the presentation was a complete disaster.
> 恕我直言
> **To put it bluntly, ...**
> It is **too big a pig**.
> It is **so big a pig**.
> Joan is **as good a doctor** as him.
> You don't know **how hard a task** it is.

many a + 单数名词（意义为复数，语序和形式为单数）
- 正常写法：Many students have failed.
- 文学/典雅写法：**Many a student has** failed.（许多学生都失败了）

让步倒装句子
Rich as he is, he is unhappy.
Rich  though he is, he is unhappy.
~~A~~ Child as he was, he knew better. // 冠词要去掉。
**Try as he might**, he could not open the door.（尽管他费尽全力想开门……）

条件虚拟句
**Should you need** any help, let me know.
**Had I known** the truth, I would have told you.

否定
**Rarely do we meet** someone so genuinely kind.
**Under no circumstances should you** touch this wire.

Bernard, are you trying to be impertinent to me?

Scotch on the rocks, plz.
> 请给威士忌加冰
> **动词 (scotch)**：彻底挫败、扑灭、终结（如 to scotch a rumor 扑灭谣言）。

He is very conscientious.
> Con-sci-en-tious 有良知的，认真的
> consci-ous
> consci-ence 良知，良心， 是非曲直
>  sci- = know
>  prescient 预知的,有先见之明的
>  omni-scient 全知的

Can ask you a hypothetical question?
That is always a good idea.
Well, suppose a minister, a purely hypothetical minister, were to be unhappy with a departmental draft of evidence to a committee.
And suppose that minister were planning to replace it with his own draft worked out with his political adviser and people from his party.
