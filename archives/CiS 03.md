## Text

The prelude
> pre-lude
> 前奏曲
> prelude -> interlude 间奏 -> postlude 尾声
> illusion (in-lude-ion) 幻觉
> illusory 虚幻的
> allude - allusion - allusive 暗示
> elude 逃避，难以理解

At this point, big flashing red lights and warning bells **should be going off** in your head.

There ought to be **some way** to **abstract out** the **commonality**  so we don't have to repeat ourselves.
> abstract out the commonality

The prelude is a module with a bunch of standard definitions that gets implicitly imported into every Haskell program.
> a bunch of = a lot of
> implicitly 隐式

It’s worth spending some time skimming through its documentation to familiarize oneself with the tools that are available.
> skim through 略读
> to familiarize yourself with sth 熟悉xxx

There’s no way to make up an element of an arbitrary type out of thin air.
> make up sth out of thin air. 凭空捏造
> arbitrary type 任意类型
