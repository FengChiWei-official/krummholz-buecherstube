## Definition

圆双元

$u^2 + v^2 = k^2$
$x^2 + v^2 = k^2$

$\int \frac {du}{v}  = \arctan (\frac{u}{v})+C = \arcsin (\frac{u}{k})+C$

$\int udv  = \frac{1}{2} u v + \frac{k^2}{2} \int \frac{du}{v}$

$\int \frac {du}{v^2} = \frac{1}{k} \tanh^{-1} (\frac{u}{k})+C = \frac{1}{k} \ln \mid \frac{a+x}{a-x} \mid + C$
>基础为裂项

>[!note] 
>如果常数是负数，则：
>$\int \frac {du}{v^2} = -\frac{1}{k} \tanh^{-1} (\frac{u}{k})+C$

双曲双元

$v^2 - u^2 = k^2$
$v^2 - x^2 = k^2$

$\int \frac {du}{v} = \ln \mid u + v \mid + C$
$\int \frac {dv}{u} = \ln \mid u + v \mid + C$

$\int udv = \frac{1}{2} u v + \frac{k^2}{2} \int \frac{du}{v}$
$\int v du = \frac{1}{2} u v - \frac{k^2}{2} \int \frac{dv}{u}$

$\int \frac {du}{v^2} = \frac{1}{k}\arctan (\frac{u}{k})+C$
