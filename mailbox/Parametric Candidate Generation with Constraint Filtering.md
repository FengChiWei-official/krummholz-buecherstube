## Definition

A common strategy in mathematics and algorithms is to **generate a parameterized family of candidate solutions** using partial constraints, and then **filter** them using the remaining constraints.

## E.G.

1. [[Binary Search on Answer or Candidate Space]]
2. [[finding the projection of the line onto the plane]]
