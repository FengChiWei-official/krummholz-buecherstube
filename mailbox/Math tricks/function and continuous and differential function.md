## Definition

### Kinds of [[Points of Discontinuity]]
We defined the kinds of [[Points of Discontinuity]].
The [[Discontinuities of the First Kind|Kind 1]] stands for [[One-side Limitation]] exists.
- they are not equal.
- they are not equal to the point.
The [[Discontinuities of the Second Kind|kind 2]] stands for [[One-side Limitation]] failed to exist or equals to infinite.

### Properties

[[Differentiability]] implies [[Continuity]].
[[Integrability]] implies [[Boundedness]].

### [[Intermediate Value Property]]

[[Anti-derivative|Original Function]] -> [[Derivative]]

According to [[Darboux 's Theorem]], there are 2 kinds of [[Points of Discontinuity]].
1. [[Oscillatory Discontinuity]] satisfies [[Intermediate Value Property]].
2. the rest don't satisfy.


### Points that continuous but not differentiable

By [[Integration]]:
[[Jump Discontinuity]] -> [[Corner Point]]
[[Removable Discontinuity]] -> [[Differential]] Point
[[Infinite Discontinuity]] -> [[Cusp]] or **Point of vertical tangency**.
[[Oscillatory Discontinuity]] -> [[Oscillatory Non-differentiable Point]]
