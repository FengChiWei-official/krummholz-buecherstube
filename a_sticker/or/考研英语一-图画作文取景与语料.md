# 考研英语一 · 图画作文：取景法与场景语料

> **原材料（or/）**：AI 整理。范围 = Part B 图画作文（20 分 / 160–200 词）的**第一段**与它的语料；P2/P3 的论证结构不在本篇。
> 判据：评分标准本来就含"内容具体、语言丰富"；模板路线把它绕过去了，于是 P1 成了空转段——P1 是全篇最集中的语言展示位，现在绕不过去。
> 挂钩：[[考研 Todo]] §学习。

## 0. 怎么用

1. §2 五问 → 30 秒出 5 个短语，这是动笔前唯一的准备。
2. §3 三个槽位各选一个（定场 / 细节推进 / 对比收口），背结构不背整段。
3. §4 按层取词，**每段亮词上限 2–3 个**，其余用准确常用词。
4. §5 先定寓意，再挑场景；画面句可改主语直接用。
5. §6 是另一类图（视角比例），规则与 §5 相反：不补环境、不读心。
6. §7 当自检表用；§8 是四周路径，验收只看表不看感觉。

## 1. 词数预算

| 段 | 词数 | 限时 | 任务 |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 描述 | 50–60 | 8–9 min | 镜头推进 + 具体细节 |
| P2 阐释 | 70–80 | 15 min | 提炼寓意 → 原因/后果两层 |
| P3 结论 | 35–45 | 8 min | 立场 + 一条可执行建议 |

描述段硬上限 60 词；超了先砍环境，保人物细节。

## 2. 取景五问（动笔前 30 秒）

1. **谁** — 年龄/身份/衣着：`a middle-aged man in a threadbare jacket`，不是 `a person`。
2. **在哪** — 空间 + 一个环境锚点（光线 / 天气 / 陈设）。
3. **干什么** — 带方式的动词：`trudge / clutch / squint at`，不是 `do / make`。
4. **哪个身体细节最说明问题** — 手、肩、眼神、姿态；生活感全部产自这一问。
5. **有没有第二个人或第二半** — 对比即寓意，是英语一最常用的构图。

五答压成三个短语，就是三句。

## 3. 句法槽位（可复用，非模板）

| 功能 | 结构 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| 定场 | On/In + 地点 + V + 主体, who/which … | On a crowded subway car, a young woman in a tailored suit stands with one hand on the strap. |
| 细节推进 | 分词前置 / with 复合 / 独立主格 | Hunched over a desk buried under sticky notes, a student looks up. / …, with three phones buzzing unanswered beside him. / …, head buried in a book, shoulders slack. |
| 对比收口 | While …; whereas … / 一句反差 | While one clutches a stack of books, the other scrolls. |
| 图注 | The caption reads: "…" | 图注原样放引号内，不改写。 |

时态：描述用现在时，图注不改，禁止用过去时讲故事。

## 4. 表达升级（按层取词）

| 不写 | 写 |
|---|---|
| walk | trudge / shuffle / stride / pace / bolt |
| sit | perch on / slump into / hunch over / sprawl across |
| hold | clutch / cradle / grip / balance / fumble for |
| look at | stare at / glance up from / squint at / fix his eyes on |
| tired | drained / bleary-eyed / worn out / shoulders slack |
| busy | swamped / juggling / up to the ears in |
| old | threadbare / weathered / patched / yellowed |
| a desk | a desk buried under paper cups and sticky notes |
| a street | a street slick with rain, neon smeared on the pavement |
| a small room | a room barely wide enough for a bed and a desk |

两条硬约束：亮词每段 ≤3；生僻词用错的扣分大于亮词加分。感官细节（光线或声音）只给一个，多了变散文。

## 5. 场景 × 寓意（体育为主，语料可迁移）

| 场景 | 画面句（可改主语） | 教育意义 | 关键表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 接力交接 | In the exchange zone, the outgoing runner stretches one arm back without breaking stride, eyes fixed on the baton. | 信任与责任传递；配合比速度更致命 | exchange zone, baton, without breaking stride, hand off |
| 长跑最后一圈 | On the last bend of an almost empty track, a lone runner gasps for air, sweat darkening his shirt, eyes on the line. | 无人喝彩时的自我驱动 | the last bend, gasp for air, eyes on the line |
| 摔倒后爬起 | A teenage skater sits on the ice, one knee scraped, while a stranger's hand reaches down from the boards. | 挫折是过程；援手来自规则之外 | scraped knee, reach down, pull himself up, dust himself off |
| 替补席 | A substitute sits at the end of the bench, a towel round his neck, following every pass, waiting to be called. | 准备与机会；位置不等于价值 | bench, towel round his neck, follow every pass, warm up |
| 水泥地练球 | On a cracked concrete court, a boy in worn sneakers practises alone — a chalk line for a goal, a brick for a net. | 条件与热爱；起点不平等 | cracked concrete, worn sneakers, chalk line for a goal |
| 满场观众看手机 | In a packed stadium, thousands of spectators look down at their screens while the match runs on unwatched. | 在场却缺席；注意力被夺走 | packed stadium, look down at screens, unwatched |
| 赛后拉起对手 | At the final whistle, the winner bends to pull the beaten opponent to his feet, one hand on his shoulder. | 竞争与尊重；赢的方式也是成绩 | final whistle, pull to his feet, one hand on his shoulder |
| 轮椅篮球 | A player in a wheelchair spins past a defender, the ball balanced on one knee, arms driving the rims. | 障碍与可能；限制不等于终点 | spin past, balanced on one knee, drive the rims |

选材原则：**能画出"身体信号"的场景才值得写**。`hands on knees`、`sleeve`、`scraped knee`、`shoulders heaving` 比任何形容词都更像"生活"，也是取景第 4 问的答案。

### 5.1 体育语料库（按镜头层）

| 层 | 词 |
|---|---|
| 场地器材 | pitch / court / track / ice, penalty area, goalmouth, crossbar, net, boards, stands, dugout, bench, chalk line, exchange zone |
| 人物 | striker, keeper, defender, referee, teammate, opponent, substitute, coach, crowd |
| 动作 | sprint, dribble, tackle, dive, save, volley, head, spin past, drive |
| **身体信号** | hands on knees, gasp for air, wipe sweat with a sleeve, shoulders heaving, limp off, arms spread wide, chin up |
| 声音光影 | whistle, the roar of the crowd, chants, the thud of the ball, shoes squeaking, floodlights, dust in the air |

身体信号这一层是核心资产，可整层搬到非体育图：`hunched over`、`hands on knees`、`wiping sweat` 换场景即用。

## 6. 视角 / 比例型图（点球双视角样例）

这类图不是写实场景，是**双框 + 一个比例差**。三条规则：

1. **先交框架**：`The drawing splits the same penalty into two panels.` 不先报框架，读者不知道有两幅。
2. **主观比例归给画面，不读心**：写 `To the striker, …` / `In his own eyes, …` / `appears to`，不写 `He thinks the keeper is big`——前者是描述，后者是编造。
3. **不补环境**：此类图没有天空、观众、草皮，编一个细节就露馅；词全给比例与姿态。

三个动词撑全段：`loom`（逼近、占满视野）、`dwarf`（使显得渺小）、`shrink to`（缩成一点）。

P1 范段（约 55 词）：

> The drawing splits the same penalty into two panels. To the striker, the keeper looms across the whole mouth, arms spread, gloves wide as a wall. To the keeper himself, the goal stretches behind him like an ocean and he shrinks to a speck against the net. Same goal, same man; only the point of view has changed.

P2 观点句（择一当主题句，别全用）：

- What the picture exposes is not a difference in the goal but a difference in how we size up the task before us.
- Fear magnifies the obstacle; focus shrinks it.
- The keeper who sees himself small has already given away half the goal.

第三条偏巧，风险是"像格言、不像论证"——后面必须跟一句原因或例证压住。

## 7. 崩法 → 自检表

| 崩法 | 症状 | 改法 |
|---|---|---|
| 流水账短句 | There is a man. He sits. He reads. | 分词 / with 复合 / 独立主格并成一句 |
| 描述超篇幅 | 超过 60 词，P2 被挤 | 砍环境，保人物细节 |
| 细节与寓意脱钩 | P2 没地方用掉那个细节 | 伏笔原则：每个细节 P2 必须被用掉一次 |
| 时态混 | 描述里出现过去时 | 统一现在时 |
| 生僻词硬凑 | 一个词自己都不确定 | 换准确常用词，亮词 ≤3 |

交卷前自检（P1 四项）：

| 项 | 标准 | 不达标怎么办 |
|---|---|---|
| 具体名词 | ≥4 | 回 §4 表，把类别词逐个换掉 |
| 方式动词 | ≥2 | 换 `do/make/look` 那几处 |
| 环境锚点 | ≥1 | 加光线或一处陈设，砍一个形容词 |
| 对比或图注 | 1 | 补收口句 |

## 8. 四周训练

| 周 | 练什么 | 量 | 自检 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 单句扩写：底句 `A man reads.`，逐层加地点 / 姿态 / 物件 / 神态 | 5 句/天 | 每层至少一个具体名词或方式动词 |
| 2 | 真题图只写 P1，8 分钟 | 7 篇 | §7 四项全绿 |
| 3 | 反刍：从 3 篇范文抠 20 组（动词, 名词）搭配，换主语自己造句 | 20 句 | 句子是自己写的，不是抄的 |
| 4 | 全篇限时 35 分钟，P1 九分钟硬停 | 3–4 篇 | 170–190 词，P2 不少于 P1 |

W1 是关键：描述卡壳几乎都不是句型问题，而是脑子里没有具体名词可取。

## 9. 迁移

- `loom / dwarf / shrink to / fill the frame` 一组词不限于体育：同一张试卷、同一个汇报台、同一个面试间，都能画成"墙"或"门"。凡"困难被放大"的图，都是这套写法。
- 体育题材的价值不在押题，而在它同时提供身体动作、对抗、环境三层语料——任何有人物的图都要用这三层。
