## Definition

All stateless components, such as [[Arithmetic Logic Unit|ALU]] , [[Floating-Point Unit|FPU]] and Bus. 
Each of them have [[Intrinsic Delay]]s, which [[Clock Cycle]]'s lower bound is subjected to it.
