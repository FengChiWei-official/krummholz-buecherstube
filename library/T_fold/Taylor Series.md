## Definition

The expansion of $f$ about $x_0$; $P_n$ is the $n$-th partial sum.

$$
P_n(x) = \sum_{i=0}^{n} \frac{f^{(i)}(x_0)}{i!}(x-x_0)^i
$$

## Hyponym

[[Maclaurin Series]]: the case $x_0 = 0$.
