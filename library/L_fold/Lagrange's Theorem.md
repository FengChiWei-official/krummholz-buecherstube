## Definition

$$
\mid G \mid \, = [G: H] \; \mid H \mid
$$
where $G$ is a [[Group]] and $H$ is its [[Subgroup]]. $[G: H]$ is the [[Subgroup Index]].

Since all of $\mid G \mid$, $[G: H]$ and $\mid H \mid$ are integers, we get:
1. $\mid G \mid \equiv 0 \mod [G: H]$
2. $\mid G \mid \equiv 0 \mod \mid H \mid$
3. $[G: H] = \frac{\mid G \mid}{\mid H \mid}$
