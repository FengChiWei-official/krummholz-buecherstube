## Definition

# [[Latin Suffix -t-or]]

## 构词公式
动词过去分词词干 (Past Participle Stem / 4th Principal Part) + -or = -tor (施事者名词)

## 核心功能
1. 表示做某事的人：actor, doctor, creator, director, victor
2. 表示执行某种功能的工具/器官：motor, generator, calculator

## 音变变体 (-sor)
- 当过去分词词干以 -s- 结尾时，变异为 -sor：
  - supervisor, professor, possessor, aggressor, sponsor

## 派生场所与身份后缀
- Place (-torium / -tory): auditorium, factory, laboratory, dormitory
- Office/Status (-torate): doctorate, directorate
