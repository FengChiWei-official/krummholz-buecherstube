## Definition

For $a, b \ge 0$ with $a + b > 0$ and $f$ convex on the interval,

$$
f\!\left(\frac{a x_1 + b x_2}{a+b}\right) \le \frac{a f(x_1) + b f(x_2)}{a+b} .
$$

In weights — with $\lambda = \frac{a}{a+b}$ —

$$
f\!\left(\lambda x_1 + (1-\lambda) x_2\right) \le \lambda f(x_1) + (1-\lambda) f(x_2) ,
$$

which is the two-point form of the [[Concavity]] definition.
