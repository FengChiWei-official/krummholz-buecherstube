## Text

`-atory` = `-ate` + `-ory`

It comes from `-orius`. the pattern `-tory` or `-sory` is from 分词.

verb. + `-ory` = adjective.
> 有带有动词带来的性质的

> explain -> explanatory
> introduce -> introductory
> contradict -> contradictory (动词来自过去分词)
> advise -> advisory
> satisfy -> satisfactory (`satisfacere`, `-ify` = `-facere`)
