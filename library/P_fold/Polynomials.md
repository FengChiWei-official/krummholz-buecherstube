## Definition

A polynomial is a finite linear combination of powers of the variable. The same polynomial expands about $0$ or about any point $x_0$:

$$
P_n(x) = \sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i x^i
$$

$$
P_n(x) = \sum_{i=0}^{n} a_i (x-x_0)^i
$$
