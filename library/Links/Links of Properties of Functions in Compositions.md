## Definition

Composition $f \circ g$ is a binary operator: it merges the properties of two functions into one. For each property the outcome depends on whether it sits on the **inner** $g$ or the **outer** $f$.

## Perspectives

### Parity — unit, absorbing element, ordinary element

- 在复合中，奇函数是单位元，偶函数吸收元，非奇非偶是普通元素。
- Detail: [[Links of Parity in Transformations]]

### Monotonicity — multiplication homomorphism on $\{+1,-1\}$

- 在复合中，单调递增，单调递减，有类似 $\{-1, 1\},*$ 的规律。
- Detail: [[Links of Monotonicity in Transformations]]

### Periodicity — the inner function decides

- 在复合中，内层是周期函数必定有周期。
- 外层是周期函数内层只讨论线性函数。
- Detail: [[Links of Periodicity in Transformations]]

### Boundedness — outer sufficient, inner not

- 有界，外面有界一定有界，里面有界不一定有界。
- Detail: [[Boundedness]]
