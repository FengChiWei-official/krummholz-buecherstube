## Definition

The same set of properties ([[Parity]], [[Monotonicity]], [[Periodicity]], [[Boundedness]]) viewed through different **operators**. The card splits by operator family into three branches.

## Perspectives

### Composition — binary operator $f \circ g$

[[Links of Properties of Functions in Compositions]] — the fixed answer of each property under composition.

### Differentiation and integration — unary operators $f \mapsto f'$, $f \mapsto \int f$

[[Links of Function Properties under Differentiation and Integration]] — whether the operator commutes with the property.

### Special transforms — unary rewrites $-f$, $f(-x)$, $|f|$, $f^{-1}$, $\ln f$

[[Links of Properties of Specific Transform]] — which class each property lands in after the rewrite.

## Distinctions

- This card cuts by **operator**; the cut by **property** is [[Links of Function Properties]].
- The properties themselves are defined in [[Properties of Functions]].
