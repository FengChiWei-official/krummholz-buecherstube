## Definition

Convexity is often tied to Jensen's inequality, so one often has to recognize the weights inside an inequality (variables subject to a sum-to-1 constraint), and the correspondence between weights and intervals — including inferring the interval backwards from the inequality's structure. Etc.

已知f(x) 有连续的二阶导数。
g(x) = f(0) (1-x) + f(1)x
在0到1上， 有 $g(x) = \frac{1-x}{1-0}f(0) + \frac{x-0}{1-0}f(1)$.适用詹森不等式。
