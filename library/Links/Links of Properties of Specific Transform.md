## Definition

One function rewritten by a unary operator — no second function joins. Each rewrite either survives a property, flips it, or loses it.

## Perspectives

### Parity

[[Links of Parity in Transformations]] — decomposition into even and odd parts, $f(|x|)$, $f(x^3)$, $f^{-1}$

### Monotonicity

[[Links of Monotonicity in Transformations]] — $-f$, $f(-x)$, $1/f$, $|f|$ ([[Absolute Function]]), $\ln f$, $e^{f(x)}$
