## Definition

> **Statement**: 
> Let $N \vartriangleleft G$. There exists an **order-preserving bijection between the set of subgroups of $G$ containing $N$ and the set of subgroups of the quotient group $G/N$ that strictly preserves inclusion, normality, and indices**:
> $$H \longleftrightarrow H/N \quad (N \le H \le G)$$  
> **Core Idea**: **"The subgroup lattice of the quotient group $G/N$ is a complete holographic projection of the upper subgroup lattice of the original group $G$."**
